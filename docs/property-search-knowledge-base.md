# GMCC Property Search — Knowledge Base

A plain-language guide to what the GMCC Property Search portal does and how to use it. Written for loan officers, and also intended to be fed to an assistant that answers questions about the platform.

---

## 1. What this portal is

GMCC Property Search is a web app that helps GMCC loan officers **find properties, figure out which GMCC loan programs those properties qualify for, and market to realtors and borrowers** — including drafting emails, making flyers, and finding refinance prospects.

Think of it as three things in one:
1. **A property + program matcher** — enter an area or an address and see which listings qualify for which GMCC programs.
2. **A marketing assistant** — draft personalized emails, generate loan-comparison flyers, and schedule follow-ups.
3. **A refinance prospecting tool** — find homeowners who look like good refi candidates from public mortgage records, and unlock their contact info.

It is a companion to the MLO Portal. From the portal, "Property Search" opens this app (and it can sign you in automatically — see Signing in).

### Who can use it
Access is limited to GMCC loan officers with a company Outlook account. If you can't get in, contact your admin.

---

## 2. Signing in

You sign in with your **GMCC Outlook (Microsoft) company account** — there's no separate password to remember.

- On the login screen, click **"Sign in with Outlook."** A small Microsoft window appears to confirm your identity, then you land in the app.
- If you came from the MLO Portal, the app usually **signs you in automatically** using your existing Microsoft session — you may not have to click anything.
- Sessions last a while, so you generally stay signed in between visits. If you're ever bounced back to the login screen, just sign in again.

If sign-in ever fails, the most reliable fix is to reload the page and click "Sign in with Outlook" again. Access is restricted to authorized loan officers; there is no self-serve signup.

## 3. The tabs at a glance

The app opens on the **CRA Check** tab. Across the top there are six tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **AI Agent** | A chat assistant that can search, match programs, draft emails, make flyers, and more — just by describing what you want. |
| **CRA Check** | Look up a single address: its details, census/CRA data, and which programs it qualifies for. |
| **Marketing** | Pull every active listing in a county (or city) and see the program matches for all of them. |
| **GPS Radius** | Find listings within a chosen distance of an address or your current location. |
| **By Program** | Start from a GMCC program and find qualifying listings in its coverage area. |
| **Refi Finder** | Find refinance prospects from public mortgage records and unlock their contact info. |

The four "search" tabs (CRA Check, Marketing, GPS Radius, By Program) all produce property results you can filter, sort, open for detail, and export to CSV. They also remember your **Recent Searches** so you can re-run one with a click.

## 4. AI Agent (AI Marketing Agent)

A conversational assistant. You type what you want in plain English and it does the work — running searches, checking programs, drafting emails, and generating flyers. When you start a new chat, suggested prompts help you get going.

**What you can ask it to do:**
- **Find properties** near an address, city, or zip — within a radius you specify.
- **Look up a specific address** and pull its details (price, beds/baths, square footage, lot size, year built, listing agent, days on market).
- **Check which GMCC programs** a property (or a batch of properties) qualifies for.
- **List GMCC programs** and where they're available.
- **Check CRA eligibility** for an address (whether it's in a qualifying census tract).
- **Look up program rules and selling points** from GMCC's internal knowledge, and ask **Admiral** (GMCC's program advisor) deeper guideline questions.
- **Search the web** for current, public information (e.g., market context).
- **Research a listing agent** to help personalize outreach.
- **Fetch a property photo** for use on a flyer.
- **Draft a personalized email** to a realtor or borrower, and **send it** through Outlook (with your approval).
- **Generate a loan-comparison flyer** (PDF) for a program + property, optionally with a photo.
- **Schedule a follow-up** — it can remind you, or auto-send a follow-up on a schedule you set.
- **Export results to CSV.**

**Good to know:**
- It **always asks for approval before sending an email** or doing anything hard to undo, and it will **ask a clarifying question** if your request is ambiguous.
- Your **email signature and the GMCC legal disclaimer are added automatically** to anything it sends.
- **Voice mode** is available if your browser supports it — you can speak your request and hear replies.
- **Chat history is saved.** Start new chats and revisit past ones from the sidebar.

## 5. CRA Check (single-address fast check)

The quickest way to evaluate one property. Enter an address (the search box autocompletes) and you get back:

- **Property details** — price, beds/baths, square footage, lot size, year built, HOA, days on market, MLS #, last sale info.
- **Census & CRA data** — census tract, MSA (metro area) name, area median income, the tract's minority percentage, and whether it's a **CRA-eligible tract** (see the glossary for LMI / MMCT / DMMCT).
- **Listing agent info** — name, phone, email, and company when available.
- **Program matches** — the GMCC programs the property is **eligible** for, **secondary/optional** programs, and **ineligible** programs, each with a short reason.
- **Photos** — pulled from the listing when available, or you can upload your own for flyers.

**From here you can:** edit/override the realtor's contact info, upload a custom property photo, select one or more programs and click **"Summary & Email"** to draft outreach, generate flyers, and compare pricing across GMCC's lending engines.

---

## 6. Marketing (bulk by county)

For working a whole area at once. Choose a **state**, **county**, and optionally a **city**, and the app pulls **every active listing** in that area and checks program eligibility for all of them.

- Results appear in a sortable table with address, price, beds/baths, type, days on market, and program-match badges.
- You'll see summary stats (total properties, average price, price range, average days on market) and live progress while it works (large counties can take a bit).
- **Filter** by program, property type, and price range; **sort** by any column; **click any row** to open the full property detail; and **download CSV** of the filtered results.

---

## 7. GPS Radius (near a point)

Find listings within a chosen distance of a place.

- Enter an **address, zip, or landmark**, or click **"Use current location"** to use your GPS.
- Choose **Area Search** (everything within a radius) or **Exact Address** (just that property).
- Set the **radius** (up to 50 miles) with the slider, and optionally filter to only properties that qualify for one or more programs.
- A map lets you fine-tune the center point.

Results are the same sortable, filterable, CSV-exportable table as the other search tabs, with program matches shown per property.

---

## 8. By Program (start from a program)

When you want to market a specific program. Pick a **GMCC program**, then a **state**, **county**, and optionally a **city**, and the app returns the listings in that area that qualify for that program — with the usual stats, filters, sorting, detail view, and CSV export.

## 9. Refi Finder (refinance prospecting)

Finds homeowners who look like strong refinance candidates using public mortgage records, then lets you unlock their contact info. This is a **paid, subscription-based** tool (see Credits & subscription below).

**How it works, step by step:**
1. **Pick a scenario (preset)** — choose one of the curated refi scenarios, or build a search from scratch. The presets are: **Cash-out candidates**, **Rate-and-term refi**, **FHA → Conventional**, **VA IRRRL**, **ARM about to reset**, and **Recent-purchase buyer's remorse**. Each comes with a description and pre-filled filters you can adjust.
2. **Set the area** — enter zip codes, or pick states/counties/cities.
3. **Adjust filters (optional)** — things like loan type, rate, equity, and property type. Preset filters show as chips you can tweak or clear.
4. **Preview (free)** — click **"Preview"** to see *how many* homeowners match. This costs nothing and returns almost instantly.
5. **Fetch (paid)** — click **"Fetch results"** to actually pull the records into a table. This spends property credits. A confirmation shows the exact cost before you're charged.
6. **Review results** — a table of matching homeowners with property address, owner name, and loan details.
7. **Unlock contact info (paid)** — contact details are hidden until you unlock them. Reveal **email** and/or **text (phone)** for individual rows, or select several rows and unlock in bulk. Each channel costs a contact credit.
8. **Fetch more** — if more matches exist than you've loaded, load the next batch. You can set how many records each fetch pulls (**batch size**, 1–100, default 25).
9. **Export** — download everything you've loaded (including any unlocked contact info) as CSV.

**Handy details:** a "cache hit" note means results came back from a shared cache (faster, same cost), and if you change filters after fetching, you'll be prompted to re-preview.

### Credits & subscription
- Refi Finder runs on a **$100/month subscription**.
- Your plan includes **5,000 property credits and 200 contact unlocks per month**, in two kinds of credits:
  - **Property credits** (5,000/month) — spent when you **Fetch** records. Fetching N records costs N property credits.
  - **Contact credits** (200 unlocks/month) — spent when you **unlock contact info**. Revealing a borrower's email is one credit and revealing their text/phone is another (so unlocking both for one person is two contact credits).
- If a channel turns out to have no data, that credit is refunded.
- **You're never charged for something you didn't get.** If a fetch or unlock is interrupted (a timeout or a hiccup on our side) after credits were taken but before the results came back, the system automatically refunds those credits — usually within the hour. When that happens you'll see an **"Auto-refunded (interrupted request)"** entry in your History, and your balance is made whole.
- Your **current balance** is shown in a pill at the top of the tab and updates as you use it.
- If you run low mid-cycle, there's an option to top up; otherwise credits refresh with your monthly renewal.
- **Access follows your billing cycle** — when your month ends, your credits refresh if your subscription renews. If you cancel auto-renew, you keep the credits you already have until the end of the current cycle, then access stops (you don't keep access indefinitely, and you aren't charged again).

## 10. Property detail view

Clicking any property (in any search tab) opens a full detail view with:

- **The basics** — price, address, status, and a button to compare pricing across GMCC's lending engines.
- **Property details** — type, beds/baths, square footage, lot size, year built, HOA, days on market, MLS #, county, last sale, price per square foot, tax-assessed value (anything missing shows "N/A").
- **Census & CRA panel** — census tract, MSA, income figures, minority percentage, and CRA-eligibility badges.
- **Photos** — a carousel from the listing, or your own uploaded photo.
- **Listing agent / builder info.**
- **Program matches** — eligible programs as cards (with the best tier, the reason it qualifies, and a **View Details** expander), plus separate sections for optional and ineligible programs.
- **Actions** — edit realtor info, upload a custom photo, and (after selecting programs) **"Summary & Email"** to draft and send outreach with flyers attached.

## 11. Emails, signatures & follow-ups

**Sending an email.** From a property (or the AI Agent), you compose an email that's pre-addressed to the realtor, with a subject and an AI-drafted or self-written body, and flyer PDFs attached automatically when you're sending flyers. You review it and send — delivery goes through Outlook.

Before an email goes out, the app:
- **Requires that you've set up your email signature** — if you haven't, it prompts you to do that first.
- **Checks the recipient's address** and warns you if it looks undeliverable.
- **Appends your signature and the GMCC legal disclaimer** automatically.

**Follow-ups.** When you send, you can turn on a follow-up:
- **Remind** — the app drafts a follow-up and reminds you to review and send it.
- **Auto-send** — the app drafts a follow-up and sends it automatically on schedule (you can review the draft beforehand).

**The Email/Follow-Up Dashboard** (envelope icon in the header) tracks everything:
- **Follow-ups** due soon (with a countdown), which you can review, edit, send early, or dismiss.
- **Sent** emails, and a **Replied** view for recipients who wrote back.
- Status badges show whether the agent replied, whether a follow-up is set to auto-send, and so on.

## 12. Flyers

A flyer is a professional **PDF handout** comparing a GMCC loan program (or several) against standard market financing for a specific property — showing the property, program terms, rates, fees, an estimated monthly payment, your and the realtor's info, and a photo.

To make one: open a property (or ask the AI Agent), pick the program(s), optionally set a photo and confirm realtor details, and generate. You can **download** the PDF or **email** it directly to the realtor. Selecting multiple programs produces a single side-by-side **loan-comparison** flyer, with a short summary (AI-generated or your own) that also flows into the email.

## 14. How program matching works

When the app says a property is eligible for a program, that decision is **rule-based, not AI guesswork**. It compares the property's facts (price, type, beds/baths, location) and its **census data** (income level, tract minority percentage, CRA-tract status) against each GMCC program's published rules.

Because it's deterministic, the same property always produces the same result, and every match comes with a plain reason (for example, a CRA program requiring the property to be in a qualifying tract within a certain price range). Results are labeled **Eligible**, **potentially eligible** (something still needs verifying), or **Ineligible** with the reason. The AI Agent uses these same rules — it doesn't invent eligibility.

## 15. Common questions

**Do I need a password?** No — you sign in with your GMCC Outlook account.

**Why can't I sign in even though pop-ups are allowed?** Reload the page and click "Sign in with Outlook" again. If it still fails, contact your the AI team.

**Is Preview in Refi Finder free?** Yes. Only **Fetch** (pulling records) and **unlocking contact info** cost credits.

**How much does Refi Finder cost?** It's a $100/month subscription that includes 5,000 property credits and 200 contact unlocks per month.

**What's the difference between property credits and contact credits?** Property credits are spent fetching records; contact credits are spent revealing a person's email or phone.

**I was charged credits but the results never loaded — did I lose them?** No. If a fetch or unlock is interrupted after credits were deducted, the system automatically refunds them (usually within the hour) and adds an **"Auto-refunded (interrupted request)"** line to your History. If you ever think a charge is wrong and it isn't refunded within a day, contact the AI team with the property/time and we can check your credit ledger.

**If I cancel, do I keep access forever?** No. Canceling stops the next monthly charge but you keep the credits you already have until your current cycle ends; after that, access stops until you subscribe again.

**Can I export results?** Yes — every search tab and Refi Finder can download results as CSV.

**Does the AI ever send emails without me?** Only follow-ups you explicitly set to "auto-send." Everything else asks for your approval first.

**Which tab should I use to check one address quickly?** CRA Check.

**Which tab pulls a whole county?** Marketing.

## 16. Glossary

- **CRA** — Community Reinvestment Act. Several GMCC programs target properties in CRA-eligible census tracts.
- **Census tract** — a small geographic area the Census Bureau uses; program eligibility often depends on the tract a property sits in.
- **LMI tract** — Low-to-Moderate Income tract (income is low or moderate relative to the area).
- **MMCT** — Majority-Minority Census Tract (a majority of residents are minorities).
- **DMMCT** — a designated majority-minority census tract.
- **MSA** — Metropolitan Statistical Area; the metro region a property belongs to.
- **AMI / MFI** — Area Median Income / Median Family Income, used to judge income level.
- **DOM** — Days on Market.
- **Preview vs. Fetch (Refi Finder)** — Preview shows the count for free; Fetch pulls the actual records and costs credits.
- **Property credit / Contact credit** — the two credit types Refi Finder spends (records vs. contact reveals).
- **Admiral** — GMCC's AI program advisor the AI Agent can consult for deeper guideline questions.
